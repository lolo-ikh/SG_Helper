EBECO — EBEC Knowledge Assistant

What is EBECO?
EBECO is the official AI knowledge assistant for EBEC (European Business Plan Competition) at ENSIA. It is a chatbot built into the EBEC Admin Hub web application that helps team members quickly find information from uploaded documents such as meeting reports, role directories, presentations, and admin documents.

Who created EBECO?
EBECO was designed and developed by Leena Ikhlef, the Vice President of EBEC 2026-2027 (formerly General Secretary in 2025-2026). It was built as part of the EBEC Admin Hub to centralize knowledge management and make it easy for team members to access information without searching through dozens of files.

How does EBECO work?
EBECO uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology. When you ask a question, it:
1. Searches through all uploaded documents using semantic similarity, full-text search, and keyword matching
2. Finds the most relevant excerpts from the documents
3. Sends those excerpts to an AI language model (Llama 3.1) which synthesizes a clear, concise answer
4. Cites the source documents so you can verify the information

What documents does EBECO have access to?
EBECO has access to all documents uploaded to the EBEC Admin Hub, including:
- Meeting reports from 2025-2026 and 2026-2027 seasons
- Team directories and organizational charts
- Roles and responsibilities documents
- Event playbooks and master plans
- Presentations and admin documents

How to use EBECO?
1. Click the blue chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of the EBEC Admin Hub
2. Type your question in the input field
3. Press Enter or click the send button
4. EBECO will search the documents and provide an answer with source citations
5. Click on source badges [1] [2] etc. to open the referenced PDF document

Who can use EBECO?
EBECO is available to all authenticated members of the EBEC Admin Hub. You need to log in with your ENSIA email account to access the chatbot.

What can I ask EBECO?
You can ask EBECO about:
- Team member roles and responsibilities across seasons
- Meeting decisions and action items
- Event planning details (Ignite, StudUps, etc.)
- Budget and financial information
- Any information contained in the uploaded documents

EBECO is best at answering questions that have clear answers in the documents. For opinions or predictions, it will tell you it doesn't have enough information.

Technical Details
- Built with React and Supabase
- Uses pgvector for semantic search (HuggingFace all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings)
- Uses PostgreSQL full-text search with OR-based keyword matching
- Powered by Llama 3.1 8B via Groq API for answer generation
- Document extraction uses pdf.js with structural parsing
- Chunks are limited to 800 characters for optimal retrieval
